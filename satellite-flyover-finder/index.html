Data sources & limitations

Orbital elements: CelesTrak (resource / EO groups). Propagation: SGP4 via satellite.js. Geocoding: OpenStreetMap Nominatim (Open-Meteo fallback). Imagery metadata: Element84 Earth Search (Sentinel-2) and USGS LandsatLook STAC.

Predictions are geometric pass estimates from public TLEs — not tasking confirmation. Seven-day replay without historical TLEs is an approximate reconstruction only. Open this page over http://localhost (not always required, but recommended) if a browser blocks network calls from file://.
